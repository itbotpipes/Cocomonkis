WHAT IS COCOMONKIS

Where coconut country meets global standards.

CocoMonkis is the premium tender coconut brand of Elite Eats Nutri Foods Pvt. Ltd. — a Surat-based enterprise bringing structured farm-to-consumer organization and export-grade standards to agricultural produce.

We source the finest tender coconuts from Gujarat’s lush farms, process them in certified quality-controlled facilities, and deliver them fresh to homes and businesses — ensuring every coconut carries the purity of origin and the precision of professional handling.
